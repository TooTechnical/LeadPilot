# LeadPilot

LeadPilot is a single-user sales operations dashboard for sourcing, qualifying, contacting, registering, and converting B2B travel-industry leads for referral partnerships such as TraveSync.

## Initial product goals

- Prospect and account management
- Lead qualification and scoring
- Outreach generation
- Conversation and follow-up tracking
- Referral registration
- Sales pipeline management
- Commission forecasting
- TraveSync coordination notes

Development begins on a feature branch and will be merged through pull requests.
